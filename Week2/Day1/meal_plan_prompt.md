# Week 2 · Day 1 — 7-Day South Indian Vegetarian Meal Plan Prompt

## ROLE

Act as a practical meal-planning assistant with knowledge of traditional South Indian vegetarian cuisine and general healthy eating principles.

Your job is to create a balanced, realistic, and easy-to-follow 7-day vegetarian South Indian meal plan for a person working towards gradual weight loss.

Do not provide medical or clinical dietary advice. The meal plan is only a general planning draft and should not replace advice from a qualified dietitian or doctor.

## FOR — EDITABLE USER DETAILS

Use the following details when creating the plan. This is the only section that should normally need editing between different users.

- Age: 35
- Activity Level: Moderately active
- Goal: Gradual weight loss
- Allergies: None
- Foods/Ingredients to Avoid: None
- Dietary Preference: Vegetarian
- Cooking Time on Weekdays: 30 minutes
- Preferred Cuisine: South Indian
- Location: Tamil Nadu, India

## CONSTRAINTS

Create a meal plan that follows all of these requirements:

1. The plan must be vegetarian.
2. The meals should be recognisably South Indian.
3. Prefer familiar South Indian dishes such as idli, dosa, sambar, pongal, upma, vegetable poriyal, kootu, rasam, curd rice, chapati, adai, sundal and similar dishes.
4. Use ingredients that are commonly available in Tamil Nadu and Indian local markets.
5. Avoid making the plan dependent on expensive or uncommon imported ingredients.
6. Do not make quinoa, avocado, protein powders, or other Western diet foods the foundation of the plan.
7. Include vegetables, pulses, lentils, whole grains and other appropriate vegetarian protein sources where practical.
8. Keep portions sensible and suitable for gradual weight loss.
9. Do not recommend extreme calorie restriction, fasting, starvation, meal skipping, or unrealistic diets.
10. Avoid excessive fried foods, sweets, sugary drinks and highly processed foods.
11. Keep weekday cooking practical and consider the stated cooking-time limit.
12. Respect all allergies, dislikes and foods to avoid listed in the FOR section.
13. Provide variety across the seven days and avoid repeating the same complete meal unnecessarily.
14. Do not assume that a particular ingredient is available if it is not commonly available locally.
15. Do not prescribe supplements or medications.

## PLAN

Create exactly 7 days of meals.

For each day include:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Snack
- Dinner

The meals should work together as a balanced daily plan rather than being selected independently.

Where appropriate, suggest simple preparation methods and practical combinations such as:

- Rice + sambar + vegetable poriyal
- Idli + sambar
- Adai + vegetable side
- Vegetable upma + chutney
- Chapati + vegetable kurma
- Millet-based traditional South Indian dishes

## PORTION GUIDANCE

Give practical household portion sizes such as:

- Number of idlis/dosas
- Cups of cooked rice
- Number of chapatis
- Cups or bowls of sambar/kootu/rasam
- Cups of vegetables
- Approximate serving size for snacks

Do not give false precision. Use reasonable household measurements such as cup, bowl, tablespoon, or number of pieces.

## AVOID

Do not include:

- Non-vegetarian foods
- Extreme dieting
- Crash diets
- Meal replacement diets
- Unnecessary supplements
- Detox drinks as a requirement
- Unrealistic restrictions
- Excessive fried foods
- Foods that conflict with the user's allergies or dislikes
- A plan dominated by Western diet foods

## OUTPUT FORMAT

Return the entire 7-day plan in ONE markdown table.

Use exactly these columns:

| Day | Meal | Dish | Portion |

Include exactly four rows for each day:

- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Snack
- Dinner

Therefore, the final table must contain exactly 28 meal rows.

After the table, provide a short section titled:

### Notes

Include no more than 5 brief practical notes about hydration, preparation, portion awareness, or meal substitutions.

Do not add lengthy explanations before the table.

## FINAL CHECK

Before providing the answer, verify that:

- All 7 days are included.
- Each day has breakfast, lunch, snack and dinner.
- There are exactly 28 meal rows.
- All dishes are vegetarian.
- The dishes are recognisably South Indian.
- Portions are included for every meal.
- Allergies and dislikes from the FOR section are respected.
- Ingredients are practical for the user's location.
- The plan does not promote extreme restriction.
- The output follows the requested table format.
