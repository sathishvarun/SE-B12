SECTION 1: ABOUT GOOGLE

Q1. What is Google?
A. Google is a technology company that develops products and services such as Google Search, Gmail, Google Maps, YouTube, Android, Chrome, Google Drive, Google Workspace, and Google Cloud.

Q2. What is Google LLC?
A. Google LLC is a company organized under the laws of the State of Delaware in the United States. Google's official Terms of Service identify Google LLC as the service provider for the services covered by those terms.

Q3. What does Google do?
A. Google provides internet, software, cloud, advertising, mobile-platform, productivity, and other technology services. The products and features available to a user depend on the product, country, account type, device, and applicable terms.

Q4. Is every Google product available in every country?
A. No. Google states that product availability can vary by country or region. Check the relevant official product page or Help Centre for current availability.

Q5. Where can I find information about Google's products?
A. Google's official products directory lists many consumer, business, and developer products:
https://about.google/intl/en/products/

SECTION 2: GOOGLE PRIVACY POLICY

Q6. What is Google's Privacy Policy?
A. Google's Privacy Policy explains what information Google collects, why it collects and uses information, how information may be shared, and how users can manage, export, or delete information. Read the full policy at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Q7. What kinds of information may Google collect?
A. Depending on the services used and the user's settings, Google says it may collect information provided by users, content created or received through its services, device and browser information, IP address and log data, activity on Google services, and location information. The exact data collected varies by service and settings.

Q8. Does Google collect information when I am not signed in?
A. Google's Privacy Policy says it may collect and use information not associated with a Google Account, including identifiers associated with a browser, application, or device. What is collected and how it is used depends on the service and circumstances.

Q9. Why does Google use information?
A. Google's Privacy Policy describes purposes that include providing and maintaining services, personalizing features and content, measuring performance, communicating with users, improving services, and protecting Google, users, and the public from fraud, abuse, security risks, and technical issues.

Q10. Does Google collect location information?
A. Google says it may collect location information depending on the services used and the settings selected. Sources may include device sensors such as GPS, IP address, activity on Google services, and information about nearby Wi-Fi access points or cell towers. The available controls and retention depend on the product and settings.

Q11. Can I control what information is saved to my Google Account?
A. Google provides account controls for reviewing and managing certain activity and privacy settings. Available controls can vary by account and product. Start at:
https://myaccount.google.com/
Privacy Checkup:
https://myaccount.google.com/privacycheckup

Q12. Can I download or delete my Google data?
A. Google provides tools to export or download certain account data and to delete selected information or services. The available options and consequences vary. Review Google's official guidance before deleting data:
https://support.google.com/accounts/
Google Takeout:
https://takeout.google.com/

Q13. Does Google share personal information?
A. Google's Privacy Policy explains that information may be shared in specified circumstances, such as when a user directs Google to share it, with domain administrators for managed accounts, with service providers, for legal reasons, or to address security and abuse. The full policy explains the circumstances and safeguards.

Q14. Does Google use cookies?
A. Google uses technologies such as cookies, local storage, and server logs to support and understand its services. Cookie use and controls depend on the service, browser, region, and settings. See:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies

Q15. Does Google use information for advertising?
A. Google's Privacy Policy explains that information and activity may be used to personalize ads or measure advertising, depending on the service and settings. Users can review relevant ad personalization controls at:
https://myadcenter.google.com/

Q16. How long does Google keep my information?
A. There is no single retention period for all Google information. Google's policy explains that retention depends on the type of information, how it is used, the user's settings, and legal or operational needs. Consult the current policy and product-specific guidance for details.

SECTION 3: GOOGLE ACCOUNT AND SECURITY

Q17. What should I do if I cannot sign in to my Google Account?
A. Use Google's official account recovery process:
https://accounts.google.com/signin/recovery
This sample chatbot cannot verify identity, reset passwords, or recover an account.

Q18. What should I do if I think my Google Account was hacked?
A. Visit Google's official account recovery and security guidance, follow the steps to secure the account, review recent security activity, and change credentials if instructed:
https://myaccount.google.com/security
Never share passwords, recovery codes, or one-time verification codes in a chatbot.

Q19. How can I improve my Google Account security?
A. Google recommends using account security tools such as Security Checkup. Depending on availability, users can review recovery options, signed-in devices, recent security activity, and two-step verification:
https://myaccount.google.com/security-checkup

Q20. Can this sample chatbot access my Gmail, Drive, Photos, or account details?
A. No. This sample FAQ has no connection to Google Account systems and cannot inspect emails, files, photos, passwords, billing information, or account activity.

SECTION 4: GOOGLE TERMS AND USER RESPONSIBILITIES

Q21. What are Google's Terms of Service?
A. Google's Terms of Service describe the terms that apply to covered Google services, including what users can expect, user responsibilities, content, and what may happen when there are problems or disputes. Some products have additional terms.
https://policies.google.com/terms

Q22. Can Google suspend or terminate access to a service?
A. Google's Terms of Service describe circumstances in which Google may suspend or terminate access, such as material or repeated violations of terms, legal requirements, or conduct that causes harm or liability. The applicable terms and process depend on the service and circumstances.

Q23. Where can I find official Google product help?
A. Use Google's official Help Centre and choose the relevant product:
https://support.google.com/

SECTION 5: SUPPORT AND CONTACT

Q24. What is Google's universal customer-support phone number or email?
A. This sample document does not provide a universal Google support phone number or email. Support options vary by product, country, and account type. Use the official Help Centre or the support route shown inside the relevant product. Be cautious of unofficial support numbers and requests for passwords or verification codes.

Q25. How can I contact Google about privacy?
A. Review the current Google Privacy Policy and the contact or privacy options provided there:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
Available contact routes may depend on the issue, region, and service.

SECTION 6: DEMO CHATBOT BEHAVIOUR (ILLUSTRATIVE)

Q26. What should the chatbot do if the answer is not in this document?
A. DEMO RULE: The chatbot should say that the available sample knowledge base does not contain enough information to answer confidently. It should not invent policies, contact details, retention periods, or account-specific facts.

Suggested response:
"I couldn't find enough information in the available Google sample FAQ to answer confidently. Please consult Google's official policy or Help Centre for the latest information."

Q27. Should the chatbot ask users for passwords or verification codes?
A. DEMO RULE: No. The chatbot should never ask users to disclose passwords, one-time passcodes, recovery codes, or other authentication secrets.

Q28. Should the chatbot claim to know a user's private Google data?
A. DEMO RULE: No. Unless a separately authorized integration has been implemented and properly permissioned, the chatbot must not claim access to Gmail, Drive, Photos, account settings, location history, or other private account data.

Q29. Should the chatbot treat this file as Google's official policy?
A. No. This is a sample RAG development document. It summarizes selected public information and is not an official Google policy, legal interpretation, or internal knowledge base.
