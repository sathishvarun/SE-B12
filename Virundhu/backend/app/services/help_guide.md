# Virundhu help guide

## What Virundhu is
Virundhu collects guest reviews for restaurants and cloud kitchens (Google, Zomato, Swiggy, Tripadvisor, Instagram,
Facebook and QR feedback) into one inbox, reads them with AI (English, Tamil and Tanglish), flags urgent complaints
(food safety, hygiene, very angry guests), drafts a reply for each, and shows reports. Built for Tamil Nadu restaurants
and chains. Based in Ganapathy, Coimbatore.

## Getting started (first 10 minutes)
1. Sign up at /register (or "Continue with Google"). The 14-day free trial starts at once, no card needed.
2. Settings → Outlets: add each branch (name, area, address, pincode, manager's name and mobile, Google Maps link).
3. Print each outlet's QR code (Settings → Outlets → QR code) and put it on tables or bills.
4. Connections → Connect Google, to bring Google reviews in automatically (see the Google section).
5. Add Zomato and Swiggy reviews by pasting, uploading a file, or uploading a screenshot (see the Zomato/Swiggy section).
The dashboard shows a "Get set up" checklist with these steps.

## Google reviews: connecting and problems
- Connections → Connect Google, sign in with the Google account that manages the Business Profile, and allow access.
- Reviews then arrive every few minutes, and replies you approve are posted back to Google.
- "Google hasn't switched on Business Profile API access" or "quota 0" or error 429: Google must approve the Virundhu
  app for the Business Profile API. This is Google's manual review and can take up to two weeks. Until then, add Google
  reviews by paste, file or screenshot, and use QR feedback.
- "Access denied" or no locations: sign in with the Google account that is an owner or manager of the Business Profile,
  and make sure the profile is verified.
- To stop: Connections → Disconnect. This deletes the stored Google access.

## Zomato and Swiggy: why there is no "Connect" button
- Zomato and Swiggy do not offer an open API for reviews to outside software, and Virundhu never scrapes or logs in to
  them (that breaks their terms and can get your listing blocked).
- Three ways to add them: (1) Add reviews → paste one review; (2) upload a CSV or Excel export; (3) upload screenshots
  from the Zomato or Swiggy partner app: the AI reads every review in the picture.
- Replies: copy the drafted reply and paste it in the Zomato/Swiggy partner app (the "Copy reply and open Zomato"
  button opens it).
- Official partner integrations need approval from Zomato/Swiggy; Virundhu will add them when available.

## QR feedback from guests
- Each outlet has its own QR code, so feedback is tagged to the right branch.
- Guests choose stars and write in any language; name and phone are optional.
- Every guest is offered the Google review button, whatever rating they give (Google bans showing it only to happy
  guests).

## Inbox, urgent alerts and replies
- Inbox tabs: Reply today (urgent), This week (1-2 star and medium), All reviews, Replied.
- Urgent alerts (food safety, hygiene, very angry) go to the owner and that outlet's manager by email (and WhatsApp once
  connected).
- Each review has an AI reply draft you can edit. Google replies post directly once Google is connected.
- If the AI couldn't read a review yet, it's sorted by its stars and read again automatically.

## Automation
- Auto-pilot: reply automatically to 4-5 star (optionally 3 star) reviews. Never promises refunds. Switch off any time.
- Holding reply: a polite "we're looking into it" after 2, 6 or 24 hours on angry public reviews.
- Ask guests for reviews: send a WhatsApp link to guests who agreed to hear from you, or connect billing software so a
  request goes after each bill. The same guest isn't asked twice in 30 days.

## Dishes and staff page
Shows every dish and staff member guests name, how often, liked vs complained, and at which branch, with the reviews.
Use it to find what the kitchen should fix first and whom to reward or train.

## Maps, directions and the website widget
- Paste each outlet's Google Maps link in Settings → Outlets → Edit. The outlet then shows "On Google Maps" and an
  "Open in Google Maps" button with Google's directions.
- "Copy review widget for your website" copies a small code for the restaurant's own website showing the true average
  rating, recent reviews and branch directions.

## Emails and WhatsApp not arriving
- Check spam/promotions. Add the sender to contacts.
- If no emails arrive at all, the email service may not be set up yet on this Virundhu installation: tell support.
- WhatsApp messages need the WhatsApp Business connection; until then review requests use one-tap WhatsApp links.

## Plans, prices and payment
- Plans: 1, 3, 6, 9 or 12 months, priced by number of outlets; longer plans and more outlets cost less per outlet;
  12 months includes free bonus months. See /pricing.
- Pay on Plan and billing (UPI, cards, net banking via Dodo Payments). Enter company name, GSTIN (optional) and billing
  address first: they print on your receipt and invoice.
- Plans never renew by themselves and you are never charged without paying yourself.
- Receipt: Plan and billing → payment history → View / print. Dodo emails the tax invoice separately.
- Paid but plan not active: wait 2 minutes and refresh. If still not active, raise a support request (Payment) with the
  payment ID or receipt number; support checks with Dodo.
- Charged twice: raise a Payment request; the extra charge is refunded in full.
- Refunds: full refund within 7 days of the first paid plan if Virundhu wasn't useful; see /refunds.

## Free trial and what happens when it ends
- 14 days, all features. Reminders 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1 days before the end by email, WhatsApp and the bell, with a payment
  link.
- If not paid, the account pauses: you can sign in and see everything, but new reviews, alerts and replies stop. Pay
  and it reopens instantly; reviews that came in meanwhile are read automatically.

## Account, sign-in and passwords
- Forgot password: Sign in → "Forgot password?" → email link.
- "Too many wrong passwords": sign-in pauses for 15 minutes for safety. Wait or reset the password.
- Passwords need 8+ characters and can't be common or contain your name, email or restaurant name.
- Team: Settings → Team: invite managers; a manager can be limited to one outlet.
- Change password: Settings → Account and privacy.

## Your data, privacy and deleting the account
- Download everything: Settings → Account and privacy → Download.
- Delete: Settings → Account and privacy → Delete my restaurant. Shows balance (always Rs 0) and unused paid days first;
  you get a confirmation email.
- Privacy Policy: /privacy. Data Processing Terms: /data-processing. Terms: /terms.

## Contact and support hours
- Help and support page inside the app (request numbers like VT-00012), or the Contact page.
- Every request is acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 15 days, usually much sooner.
