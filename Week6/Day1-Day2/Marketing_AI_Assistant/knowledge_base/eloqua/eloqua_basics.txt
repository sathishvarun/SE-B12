Oracle Eloqua Marketing Automation

Oracle Eloqua is a marketing automation platform used for campaign management, lead nurturing, email marketing and marketing operations.

Campaign Canvas:
Campaign Canvas allows marketers to design and execute marketing campaigns using assets such as emails, landing pages, forms and decision steps.

Program Canvas:
Program Canvas is used to build programs that manage contacts through a sequence of steps based on program membership and defined rules.

Contact Management:
Eloqua stores contact information and engagement data. Marketers can use segmentation to target relevant audiences.

Lead Nurturing:
Lead nurturing uses automated campaigns to deliver relevant communications to prospects based on their interests, behavior and engagement.

CRM Integration:
Eloqua can integrate with Salesforce CRM to synchronize relevant contact, account and lead information between marketing and sales systems.
