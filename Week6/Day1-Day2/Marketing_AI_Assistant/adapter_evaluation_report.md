3. **Email Notification**: The registrar can also send an email to the attendee's registered email address explaining the cancellation and providing a reason for it (e.g., they were unable to attend due to unforeseen circumstances).

2. **How It Works:**

   - **Lead Generation:** Customers can be automatically identified through Salesforce and then promoted to Eloqua for further nurturing or conversion efforts.
   - **Sales Pipeline Tracking:** Sales pipelines in Salesforce are mapped to Eloqua, enabling real-time monitoring and optimization of sales processes.

3. **Benefits:**
   - **Enhanced Customer Engagement:** By integrating Salesforce and Eloqua, companies can create more personalized experiences for customers, leading to higher satisfaction rates and repeat business.
   - **Improved Lead Generation:** Salesforce’s data collection allows for better targeting and segmentation of leads, which can increase the likelihood of converting them into customers.
