# SK Soft Technology Finance: Client Invoices, Payments and Employee Expenses

## Client invoicing
- Time-and-material (T&M) projects are invoiced monthly on the 3rd working day, based on approved timesheets.
- Fixed-price projects are invoiced on each milestone the client signs off.
- Payment terms are Net 30 days from the invoice date unless the contract says otherwise.
- Every invoice carries the PO number, the GSTIN of both parties and the SOW reference.

## Duplicate or wrong charge on an invoice
1. The client shares the invoice number and the payment reference (UTR or transaction ID).
2. Finance checks the bank statement and the payment gateway within 2 business days.
3. If a duplicate payment is confirmed, the extra amount is refunded within 5 business days, or adjusted against the next invoice if the client prefers.
4. A credit note is issued for every refund or adjustment.

## Employee expense claims and reimbursements
1. Submit claims in the Expense Portal within 30 days of the expense.
2. Attach a clear photo or PDF of each bill. Claims above Rs 5,000 need project manager approval.
3. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.
- Travel limits for client visits: hotel up to Rs 4,000 per night in metro cities and Rs 2,500 elsewhere; daily food allowance Rs 1,000; local cab travel is reimbursed at actuals with receipts.
- Onsite (abroad) assignments get a daily allowance as per the country table in the Travel Policy.

## Vendor payments
- Vendor invoices are paid within 45 days of receiving a valid invoice with a PO number.
