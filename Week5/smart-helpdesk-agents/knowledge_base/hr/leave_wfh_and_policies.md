# SK Soft Technology HR: Leave, Hybrid Work, Onboarding, Appraisals and Workplace Policies

## Leave types per year
- Casual leave: 12 days. Sick leave: 10 days. Earned (privilege) leave: 18 days.
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks. Paternity leave: 10 working days.
- Up to 10 unused earned leaves can be carried forward to the next year.

## How to apply for leave
1. Log in to the HR Portal (SK People) and open Leave > Apply.
2. Choose the leave type and dates and add a short reason.
3. Casual and earned leave must be applied at least 3 working days in advance; inform your project manager so client deliverables are planned.
4. Sick leave of more than 2 days needs a medical certificate.
5. Your reporting manager approves or rejects within 2 working days.

## Joining documents to submit to HR
Upload these in SK People within 3 working days of joining (scanned copies; bring originals for verification on day 1):
1. Signed offer letter and appointment letter.
2. Identity and address proof: Aadhaar card and PAN card (passport if available).
3. Education certificates: 10th, 12th and degree/diploma mark sheets and certificates.
4. Experience documents from previous employers: relieving letter, experience letter and last 3 months' payslips.
5. Two recent passport-size photos.
6. Filled forms in SK People: personal details, emergency contact, nominee details and the code of conduct acceptance.
- For payroll details (bank account, PF/UAN, tax declaration) see the Finance team's payroll checklist.

## Hybrid work (work from home)
- Employees work from the office at least 3 days a week and may work from home up to 2 days a week.
- Client-facing projects may require more office days as per the client contract.
- Full remote work for longer periods needs approval from the delivery head and HR.

## Onboarding for new joiners
- Day 1: laptop handover, ID card, HR induction and policy acceptance in SK People.
- Week 1: mandatory security and POSH trainings, and project allocation by the resource management team.
- A buddy from the project team is assigned to every new joiner for the first 30 days.

## Appraisals and bench
- The appraisal cycle runs every April, with a mid-year review in October.
- Employees not allocated to a project (bench) must complete assigned upskilling courses and stay available for client interviews.

## Notice period
- 60 days for all employees, and 90 days for managers and above.

## Workplace complaints
- Complaints about harassment, discrimination or unsafe behaviour are handled confidentially by the Internal Committee (IC) under the POSH policy.
- Email ic@sksoft.example or speak to any HR business partner. The committee responds within 2 working days.
