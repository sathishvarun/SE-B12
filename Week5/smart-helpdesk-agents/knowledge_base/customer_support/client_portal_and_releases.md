# SK Soft Technology Client Portal, Access and Releases

## Client portal login problems
1. On the portal login page click Forgot Password and enter your registered work email.
2. Use the reset link within 30 minutes.
3. The new password needs at least 12 characters with upper case, lower case, a number and a symbol.
4. After 5 wrong attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes.
5. Single sign-on (SSO) users must reset their password with their own company IT team.

## Adding or removing client users
- Only the client's named admin can request new portal users or removal of users, by raising a P4 ticket.
- Requests are completed within 1 business day.

## Reporting a bug
- Raise a ticket with the steps to reproduce, the expected result, the actual result, the browser or app version and screenshots.
- Confirmed bugs are given a bug ID and planned into a release based on severity.

## Releases and maintenance windows
- Planned releases happen every second Saturday between 10 PM and 2 AM IST.
- Clients are informed by email 5 business days before each release, with release notes.
- Emergency hotfixes for P1 issues can be deployed at any time after client approval.
