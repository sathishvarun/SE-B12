# SK Soft Technology Client Support and SLA Policy

## Severity levels and response times
- P1 Critical: production is down or no users can log in. First response within 15 minutes, 24x7. Target fix or workaround within 4 hours.
- P2 High: a major feature is broken for many users, no workaround. First response within 1 hour. Target fix within 1 business day.
- P3 Medium: a feature is broken but a workaround exists. First response within 4 business hours. Target fix within 5 business days.
- P4 Low: questions, how-to requests and small UI issues. First response within 1 business day. Fixed in a planned release.

## How to raise a support ticket
1. Log in to the SK Soft Client Support Portal and click New Ticket.
2. Choose the product, the environment (Production, UAT or Dev) and the severity.
3. Describe the problem, the steps to reproduce it, the time it started and attach screenshots or error logs.
4. You receive a ticket number by email immediately. All updates are posted on the ticket.
5. For a P1 outage, also call the 24x7 support hotline so the on-call engineer is paged at once.

## Production outage (P1) process
- The on-call engineer joins a bridge call within 15 minutes and shares updates every 30 minutes.
- The client's account manager and the delivery head are informed automatically.
- After the fix, a Root Cause Analysis (RCA) report is shared within 3 business days.

## Escalation matrix
- Level 1: Support engineer on the ticket.
- Level 2: Support team lead, if there is no response within the SLA time.
- Level 3: Delivery manager and account manager for repeated SLA misses.

## SLA service credits
- If the monthly uptime falls below the 99.5% committed in the contract, the client receives a service credit of 5% of that month's support fee for every 0.5% below target, up to 25%.
