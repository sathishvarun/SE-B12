# SK Soft Technology Marketing: Brand, Case Studies, Webinars and Social Media

## Brand guidelines
- Primary colour: SK Soft Blue #1D4ED8. Accent: Signal Orange #FF6B1A. Text: Graphite #1F2937.
- Fonts: Poppins for headings, Inter for body text.
- Do not stretch, recolour or add effects to the logo. Keep clear space around it equal to the height of the letters SK.
- Use the official slide template from the Marketing Hub for all client presentations.

## Publishing a client case study
1. Fill in the Case Study Request form in the Marketing Hub with the project summary, technology used and measurable results (for example cost saved, time reduced).
2. Get written approval from the client to use their name and logo. Without approval, the case study is published as anonymous ("a leading bank").
3. The content team drafts the case study within 7 working days.
4. The delivery manager checks technical accuracy and the account manager gives final sign-off.
5. Approved case studies go on the website, LinkedIn and sales decks.

## Hosting a webinar or tech talk
- Submit the webinar request at least 21 days before the event with the topic, speakers, target audience and date.
- Marketing creates the registration page and sends reminders 7 days, 1 day and 1 hour before the event.
- A dry run with all speakers is mandatory 2 days before the event.
- Registrant and attendee leads are shared with Sales within 2 working days after the event.

## Social media and blogs
- Only the marketing team posts on official SK Soft Technology accounts.
- Employees can write technical blogs; submit drafts to the content team for a 3 working day review.
- Never share client names, code, architecture diagrams or project details publicly without approval.
