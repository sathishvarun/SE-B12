# SK Soft Technology Sales: Rate Card, Discounts, RFPs and Contracts

## Standard monthly rate card (India offshore, per resource)
- Junior developer (0-2 years): Rs 1,80,000 per month.
- Developer (3-5 years, Java, .NET, Python, React): Rs 2,60,000 per month.
- Senior developer / tech lead (6+ years): Rs 3,60,000 per month.
- QA engineer: Rs 2,00,000 per month. DevOps / cloud engineer: Rs 3,00,000 per month.
- Onsite resources are priced separately by the pricing team.

## Discount approval limits
- Account managers can approve up to 5% discount.
- Sales heads can approve up to 10%.
- Discounts above 10%, or any deal with 10 or more resources for 12 months or longer, need approval from the pricing committee (decision within 3 working days).
- Volume guide: 5 to 9 resources for 12 months or more can receive up to 7%.

## Responding to an RFP
1. Log the RFP in the CRM within 1 working day and share it with the pre-sales team.
2. Hold a bid / no-bid meeting within 2 working days.
3. Send clarification questions to the client before their question deadline.
4. Pre-sales prepares the solution and effort estimate; pricing team prepares the commercials.
5. The delivery head and sales head review the final proposal at least 2 days before submission.

## Contracts
- An NDA must be signed before receiving any confidential client documents.
- Every engagement needs a signed MSA (master agreement) and a SOW (statement of work) before work starts.
- Proof of Concept (POC) work of up to 2 weeks can be offered free with sales head approval.
