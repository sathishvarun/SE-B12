Scheme Name: Chief Minister Solar Powered Pump Sets Scheme
Department: Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
Eligibility: Small and marginal farmers with verified land records and no existing subsidized grid pump set.
Beneficiary: Small Farmers, Marginal Farmers
Benefit: 70 percent subsidy on installation of 5 HP to 10 HP solar pumps.

Scheme Name: Integrated Farming System (IFS)
Department: Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
Eligibility: Wetland and garden land farmers residing in selected agro-climatic zones.
Beneficiary: Small Landholders
Benefit: Financial assistance up to Rs. 50,000 for integrating crop cultivation, milch animals, and apiary.
